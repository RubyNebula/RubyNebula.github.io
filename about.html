Who Is Ruby Nebula?

I am a reader, writer, dreamer, romantic, and lifelong student of the world. I am forever curious about people and the peculiar, beautiful, complex world we share.

I am empathetic and compassionate, and wish to be kind to everything that breathes. I want to help others, make people smile, and lend hope.

I love learning, reading, writing, travelling, and discovering new places, ideas, experiences, and wonders. I believe that is the nature of humanity. It is why we have legs instead of roots.

Why I Write

I write because knowledge-sharing is ancestral and human.

Writing gives humanity a way to explore and discover insights that don't always have simple answers — insights about the mind, spirituality, community, culture, justice, identity, and all the strange things in the world.

Some of my writing explores the tragic parts of humanity - crime, violence, suffering, and injustice. Other pieces explore identity, gender, sexuality, culture, and the human experience. I write because people deserve a voice.

The stars light our way.

I am inspired by the universe at night.

There is something inspiring about gazing at a starry night sky filled and remembering how beautiful and mysterious the universe truly is. We are all made of stardust.

I find that thought both humbling and exhilarating.

I want to live with that same sense of wonder — to keep reading, exploring, questioning, travelling, discovering, and searching for beauty. I am an adventurer.
